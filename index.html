Travel Tips for a Smooth Journey

Carry Valid ID

Keep one original photo ID such as Aadhaar, passport or driving licence.

Reach Early

Arrive at least 30 minutes before departure to find your platform.

Pack Smart

Free luggage limit is 40 kg in SL and 3A, 50 kg in 2A and 70 kg in 1A.

Pre-order Meals

Pre-book meals on long routes to avoid station rush.

Keep PNR Handy

Note your PNR from the e-ticket to check chart status quickly.

Stay Safe

Keep valuables close and note emergency helpline 139.
