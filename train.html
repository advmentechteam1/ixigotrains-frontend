Train Travel Tips

Carry Valid ID

Keep one original photo ID such as Aadhaar, passport or driving licence.

Reach Early

Arrive at least 30 minutes before departure to find your platform.

Pack Smart

Free luggage limit is 40 kg in SL and up to 70 kg in 1A.

Stay Safe

Keep valuables close and note emergency helpline 139.

Train Booking FAQs

Advance reservation opens 60 days before the journey date, excluding the date of travel.
Tatkal is a last-minute quota that opens one day before the journey at an additional charge.
Children under 5 travel free without a berth. Children aged 5–11 can be booked half fare with or without a berth.
Fully cancelled trains are refunded automatically to your original payment mode.
