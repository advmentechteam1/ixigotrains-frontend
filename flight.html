Airport Tips

Reach Early

Arrive 2 hours before a domestic flight and 3 hours before an international one.

Valid ID

Carry a government photo ID such as Aadhaar, passport or driving licence.

Baggage Limit

Cabin bags are usually limited to about 7 kg; check your fare rules.

Web Check-in

Check in online to save time at the airport counter.
