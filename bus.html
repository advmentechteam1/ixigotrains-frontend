Bus Travel Tips

Reach Early

Arrive at the boarding point 15–20 minutes before departure.

Carry ID

Keep a valid photo ID and your e-ticket on your phone.

Luggage

Most operators allow about 15–20 kg per passenger; check with the operator.

Save Contact

Note the driver or operator number shown on your ticket.
